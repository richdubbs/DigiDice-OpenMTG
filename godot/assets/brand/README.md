their own versions; replace them with your own assets.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `digidice-mark-transparent.png` | The DigiDice logo with a transparent background (for dark surfaces) |
| `sleeves/sleeve-<id>.png` | Deck sleeves: the DigiDice card back in the mana colors, one for every color and every combination (31), plus the classic colors (`sleeve-classic.png`); 372x520 |
| `sleeves/sleeves.json` | The list of sleeves and their colors, read by the deck boxes |

Brand colors: blue `#1FB8FF` (deep `#0098F0`), magenta `#F500F5`, white `#E8E8F0`, on black.
