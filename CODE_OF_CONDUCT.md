# Code of Conduct

OpenMTG is a hobby project made by and for people who enjoy the game. Keep it a good place to be.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree about code and rules, not about people.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind (including racist, sexist, homophobic,
  transphobic, ableist, or ageist content). No sexualized content.
- Respect people's time: keep issues and reviews focused and constructive.
- Respect intellectual property: do not contribute card images, scans, bulk card data, or other
  third-party content to the repository.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments, and may block people who do not follow
this code. To report a problem, open an issue marked "conduct" or contact a maintainer privately.
